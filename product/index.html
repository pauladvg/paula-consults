Product that works for people and for the business.

I’ve worked at tech startups in Europe and the US since 2020. I started on the business side, across operations, support, strategy and growth, then moved into product in 2023. Today I work in product at a global wellness tech platform.

The creative thinking my background has given me lets me solve problems from unexpected angles. To me, a good product helps users and creates a net-positive impact on the day-to-day lives of others, while also generating revenue to fuel the company that keeps it all going.

Associate Product Manager

Oct 2025 – Present

Strategy · Roadmapping · KPIs

I own the product strategy and roadmap for B2C and B2B, with 40M+ eligible users and 10+ corporate clients.

UX/UI design · A/B testing · Market research

I lead the end-to-end redesign of our web and mobile app. I also own the UX/UI direction and architecture. At times I act as the product designer myself, using AI to speed up the design process. I’m in charge of user research and benchmarking, and I keep design and engineering aligned.

Backlog · Agile · Scrum · Sprint planning

I lead a development team of 3. I restructured the backlog so we could plan with less churn and deliver more of what mattered each sprint: I rebalanced the workload across the team, sharpened priorities and wrote clearer user stories and task descriptions. In three months, sprint completion rose by 25% and we started consistently meeting our sprint goal deadlines.

AI · Automation · Data-driven decision making

I built AI-powered workflows that process documents such as new partner contracts, extract the key data and feed it into our internal tools, automating profile creation, documentation and reporting. This cut manual operations and support work by 30%.

Partnerships · Pricing · Stakeholder management

I’m co-leading the launch of a €90K growth experiment with DragonPass across the customers of several international banks, inside a $1B travel benefits ecosystem. I’m shaping the test design: choosing which user segments to target and sizing the eligible pools from aggregated, anonymized partner data, since we don’t access individual customers. I also coordinate the partners and set up how we’ll measure the results.

Roadmapping · Prioritization · Stakeholders

Data · Conversion · Revenue · SQL · KPIs
