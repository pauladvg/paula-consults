About me

I'm a Product Manager with four years of experience, but product is only part of the picture. I've led teams across operations, support, strategy and growth in Europe and the US, mostly in fast-moving tech startups.

Before that, I spent years in the music industry and other artistic fields, managing artists, booking tours with no agent, organizing exhibitions and running events. It taught me to find original solutions when the usual ones aren't available.

That mix of strategic thinking, resilience and creativity is what led me here. Now I help growing companies whose teams and processes haven't caught up with their growth to figure out exactly what's blocking them and put a concrete plan in place to move forward.

Work

A selection of projects where I helped teams get organized, find clarity and move forward.

Consultant for a SAP agency

Started
From August 2025
Duration
3–6 months
Industry
Education

The client:

A SAP consultancy agency that wanted to offer a more practical and accessible approach to SAP education for students and professionals worldwide.

What I did:

I helped this agency build a hybrid training school combining online modules, in-person bootcamps, and internship opportunities for people starting in SAP.

I analyzed the company, the competitors, the goals and the project, and set up the roadmap. I developed guidelines across legal, operational, educational and technical areas, drafted contracts for trainers, mapped out course structures across key SAP modules, and built the onboarding process for instructors. I listed everything they were missing, such as venues for in-person sessions, schedules, course pacing and live support, and helped them fill those gaps, setting the foundation for this to scale into a real product.

Consultant for an IT platform

Started
From January 2023
Duration
6+ months
Industry
Fintech & Logistics

The client:

Equipped, an outsourced IT team for businesses without one, handling everything from buying and leasing devices to software, onboarding, compliance and ongoing support.

What I did:

I consulted for Equipped on operations, product and customer success.

I analyzed why these layers were causing friction, organized and documented the core logistics processes, and implemented an internal tracking system to monitor the full lifecycle of every order. I worked closely with the founding team to define workflows, define the support role, and coordinate across hardware vendors, internal teams and external partners like Apple Consultants. I also improved key parts of the billing process, so each customer, whether on a recurring plan or a one-time setup, was invoiced accurately and on schedule.
