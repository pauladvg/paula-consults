About me, the long version.

I’m a product manager and business consultant, but I didn’t start out in tech. I graduated in Spanish Language and Literature and hold two master’s degrees, in Cultural Studies and in Education. For some years I worked in those fields, including academia, until I decided to pivot.

Early years in tech

I’d been doing consulting on the side since 2018, first for artists and later for companies. But I purposefully landed in tech in 2020, and since then I’ve worked at startups in Europe and the US. I started in Customer Support at Upgraded, a role that soon grew into something in between support, operations and customer experience, and I went on to become Support Manager, leading a team of four.

For years I’d been reporting bugs, suggesting improvements and pointing out growth opportunities. When a former manager recommended me for a product role, he said I was good at understanding what’s going on, deciding what matters most and making sure it gets done. He believed I could grow as a PM, and that’s how I moved into product.

Product and consulting

Since moving into product, I’ve taken courses in product, project management and management consulting to back up my experience with theory, and they’ve given me a new way of looking at the things I use every day.

Today I work in product at BODDY, a global wellness tech platform, and I take on consulting projects alongside it.

I like working closely with everyone involved in a product. I’ve worked with support teams, developers, banks, retailers and founders, so I’m used to translating between people who don’t speak the same lingo. Sometimes I’ve even translated for real: I’m a native Spanish speaker and fluent in English.

You can see more about my work in each area on the Product and Consulting pages.

I still love the cultural world (literature, music, education), and I’ll always be open to working on a product in that space. But I’m also always up for a new challenge, so even if it’s unknown territory, I’ll be excited to learn.

You can find my full experience on LinkedIn. If you’d like to work together, get in touch.
