Consulting to find what’s blocking your growth.

I help growing companies figure out what’s blocking their progress. Then we put a concrete plan in place to move forward. We’ll start by identifying the real problem, because the visible one is rarely the root cause. I’ll interview your team at every level, review your processes, data and finances, and map where things break down.

I’ll ask uncomfortable questions and push back on assumptions to figure out a solution for your situation. If this sounds like something you might need, let’s talk.
